Questions

FAQ for QR creation on iPhone.

Can I create a QR code with a logo on iPhone?

Yes. inQRedible lets you add a center logo, choose module and eye styles, adjust colors and export the result from your iPhone.

Does inQRedible generate Wi-Fi QR codes?

Yes. The Wi-Fi template supports open, WEP and WPA/WPA2 networks, hidden networks and the standard QR Wi-Fi format.

Are my QR codes uploaded to a server?

QR content, logos, selected images and history are processed locally for product functions and are not uploaded to inQRedible servers to generate codes. Optional consented analytics and diagnostics are separate and exclude QR, scan, clipboard and user-entered payloads.

What barcode formats can I create?

inQRedible supports EAN-13, UPC-A, Code 128 and PDF417 generation, plus scanning for QR codes and compatible barcode formats.
